About AgriLearn

A simple learning platform created to help Agricultural Science students study, research and practise what they learn.

Why AgriLearn?

Agricultural Science is a practical subject that connects science, farming, the environment and everyday life. AgriLearn was created to make it easier for students to revise important concepts and find useful learning resources in one place.

The website is designed for secondary school learners studying Agricultural Science, with content organised around topics such as soil, crop production, livestock, farm management and agricultural economics.

Students can use the Research Hub to revise topics, use the Study Materials section to find additional resources, and practise their knowledge with the Quiz Arena.

What students can do

  • Explore topics Use the Research Hub to browse Agricultural Science topics according to grade level.
  • Find study resources Discover textbooks, libraries and online resources that can support revision.
  • Practise knowledge Use the Quiz Arena to answer questions and receive feedback after each answer.
  • Learn at your own pace Students can return to the website whenever they need extra revision or practice.

Our focus

AgriLearn focuses on making Agricultural Science learning simple, organised and accessible to students.

The platform encourages learners to connect classroom knowledge with real agricultural practices and the farming environment around them.
